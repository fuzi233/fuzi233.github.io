Research & Collaboration

I am currently studying how to improve the chances of sampling correct solutions from LLMs on difficult problems, particularly when correct answers are rare and rewards are sparse.

I am also investigating what test-time reinforcement learning actually improves: exploration, exploitation, or both. I would be happy to connect with others working on these questions and explore potential collaborations.
